Statement & Evidence composition

va-spec-source.yaml

A top-level Statement makes a claim; the distinct EvidenceLine class captures a scored, directional argument for it (via hasEvidenceLines) and cites the information assessed as evidence (via hasEvidenceItems) — a StudyResult, a DataItem, or another Statement playing an «EvidenceItem» role. A Statement is not itself an Evidence Line.
