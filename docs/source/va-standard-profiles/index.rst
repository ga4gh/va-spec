VA Standard Profiles
!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!

**VA Standard Profiles** are models that implementers can use to represent and exchange a specific type of variant knowledge in their data systems. They are defined as specializations of the :ref:`Statement<Statement>`, :ref:`EvidenceLine<EvidenceLine>`, :ref:`Proposition<Proposition>`, or :ref:`Study Result<StudyResult>` classes in the VA Core Model.  

 * :ref:`Statement Profiles<statement-profiles>` support discrete assertions of **general knowledge** about a variant (e.g. a pathogenicity classification).  

 * :ref:`Evidence Line Profiles<evidence-line-profiles>` describe **how information is interpreted as evidence** supporting or disputing a possible fact (Proposition) that may ultimately be asserted about a variant (e.g. how results from an allele frequency analysis are interpreted to support the allele’s pathogenicity).

 * :ref:`Proposition Profiles<proposition-profiles>` are **used within Statement and Evidence Line profiles** - where they encapsulate the **semantics of a particular kind of possible fact** that may be asserted or evaluated in a Statement, or against which evidence may be assessed in an Evidence Line (e.g. pathogenicity propositions cpature a possible fact that some variant is causal for some disease).

 * :ref:`Study Result Profiles<study-result-profiles>` capture collections of **data items about a variant**, from a particular study or analysis dataset (e.g. allele frequency data about a specific variant in a particular cohort from the gnomAD dataset).

Profiles are authored as YAML-based information models (**Source YAML**), from which machine-readable **JSON Schema** and user-facing **Web Documentation** are automatically generated by `GKS Metaschema Tooling <https://github.com/ga4gh/gks-metaschema>`_.  These artifacts are accessible for each VA Standard Profile through the links below.
